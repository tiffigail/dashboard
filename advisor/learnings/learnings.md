# Learnings

*This file is for the advisor to maintain, not Abi. It records observations, patterns, breakthroughs, and things Abi has said that matter — across conversations — so the advisor gets smarter about her over time rather than starting fresh each session.*

---

## How to use this file

Each entry has a fixed structure. Be disciplined about it. A wall of unstructured notes becomes unreadable; a structured log stays useful even when it gets long.

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — [short title]

- **What I noticed:** [the observation, in one or two sentences]
- **What prompted it:** [the conversation moment or context]
- **Tentative or confirmed:** [tentative pattern / confirmed pattern]
- **Implication for future conversations:** [what this should change about how I show up next time, if anything]
```

Confirmed patterns are observations Abi has explicitly verified or that have repeated enough that the pattern is unmistakable. Tentative patterns are first-noticings that need more data before being treated as durable. Move entries from tentative to confirmed by editing the field, not by creating a new entry.

When entries become very old or stale, archive them at the bottom under `## Archive` rather than deleting. Patterns can become relevant again.

---

## What's worth recording

- **Specific phrases she uses repeatedly** that the advisor should mirror or recognize as signals
- **Patterns of dysregulation** — when does she redline, what precedes it, what works to bring her back
- **Patterns of flow** — when is she in the groove, what conditions support it
- **Things she says that matter** — phrases, realizations, declarations that should not be lost
- **Failures of the advisor** — moments when the advisor's response missed her, what landed wrong, what was needed instead
- **Successes of the advisor** — moments when something worked unusually well, so it can be repeated
- **Shifts in her language or framing** that indicate a real change in her thinking
- **Cross-axis observations** — when something in one axis is clearly causing something in another

## What's NOT worth recording

- Generic restatements of things already in `profile.md`, `system.md`, or `spiritual.md`
- Anything that is just a fact about a single conversation rather than a pattern
- Praise of Abi for its own sake (this is not a fan log)
- The advisor's own self-reflection that doesn't translate to changed behavior next time

This file is kept as human-readable reference and legacy context. New entries should go to Firestore, not here.
