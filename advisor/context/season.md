## How to update this document

1. At the top of each month (or whenever the season shifts), open this file.
2. Rewrite both sections to reflect what's actually true right now — not what you wish were true, not what you said last month.
3. Keep it short. ~100 words is the discipline. If you write 500 words, the advisor will weight it equally with `profile.md` and the signal will get lost.
4. Update the date.
5. If something major has shifted (new job ending, family event, health change, project completion), make sure the advisor learns about it here — not just in conversation, where it might not get encoded into context.

## A note on what this document is for

The other context documents are slow-moving. They describe who you are, what you believe, the architecture you operate within, and how you want to be met. They should not change much.

**This document is the fast-moving layer.** It tells the advisor what to weight more heavily right now. It is the difference between him knowing you in the abstract and knowing what is actually in front of you this month.

Treat the rewrite as a small monthly ritual. It can be part of your weekly reset, the first weekly reset of each new month. You already do the practice of "premise writing" when stressed — this is the calmer, scheduled version of that. A premise for the season rather than for the moment.
